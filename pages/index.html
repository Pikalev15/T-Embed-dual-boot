Safety first

Experimental firmware: not yet physically boot-tested. Flashing rewrites the bootloader and partition layout. Back up your SD card and ensure you can recover your device over USB.

Don't use the upstream single-OS web flasher, qT-Embed Full Flash, or firmware OTA updater on this dual-boot layout. This tool writes both OSes and resets the boot selection to Flipper. NVS and Bruce SPIFFS are not intentionally erased.
